About Kabankalan Catholic College

A Catholic educational institution committed to providing quality education, nurturing faith, developing strong values, and preparing students for a brighter future.

  • Providing quality education that develops knowledge, skills, and creativity.
  • Promoting Christian values, discipline, respect, and responsibility.
  • Preparing students to become future leaders and professionals who contribute to society.

At Kabankalan Catholic College, we believe that education goes beyond the classroom. We encourage students to discover their potential, strengthen their faith, and pursue excellence.
